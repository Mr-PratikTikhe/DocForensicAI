# Public demo PDF sources

These sample PDFs are provided for the DocForensic AI public demo and are each under 4 MB.

- `planck-origin-quantum-theory.pdf` — Max Planck, *The Origin and Development of the Quantum Theory*. Downloaded from [Project Gutenberg ebook 33663](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/33663). Project Gutenberg identifies this work as public domain in the United States.
- `einstein-relativity.pdf` — Albert Einstein, *Relativity: The Special and General Theory*. Downloaded from [ibiblio's Einstein collection](https://www.ibiblio.org/ebooks/Einstein/index.html), which identifies the original book as public domain in the United States and notes that copyright status can differ by country.

The demo is read-only. Visitors must select one or more documents in the sidebar before asking a question; retrieval is limited to those selected demo documents.
